Interactive Petal Sculpture
brush the rings to twirl them

Controls

Mouse and touch

Move across the rings
Twirl each ring you pass over. Faster strokes spin harder.
Click or tap a ring
Give it a sharper flick.
Click and drag
Rotate the view around the sculpture.
Scroll or pinch
Zoom in and out.
Colour swatches
Change the metal the rings are made of.
Double-click
Reset the view to the front, level, at the starting distance.

Turn the slow automatic orbit on or off.
↑ ↓
